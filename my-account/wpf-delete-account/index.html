Account & Data Deletion

Permanent Account & Data Deletion Request

In compliance with Google Play Store User Data and Privacy Policies, Alifeo provides a clear mechanism for users to request permanent deletion of their account and all associated personal records. This action is irreversible.

What data will be permanently deleted?

Once your deletion request is verified and processed, the following information will be completely and permanently erased from our production databases within 30 days:

  • Identity & Profile Details: Your full name, registered email address, mobile phone number, profile photo, and security credentials.
  • Addresses & Geolocation: All saved delivery addresses, GPS coordinates, pinned locations, and contact person details.
  • Shopping History & Lists: Shopping cart items, wishlist records, past order logs, product reviews, and browsing activity.
  • Wallet & Payment Tokens: Tokenized payment identifiers, saved UPI handles, promotional vouchers, discount codes, and referral bonuses.
  • Device & Session Tokens: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) push tokens, session authentication tokens, and device identifiers.

Data Retention & Legal Compliance

Certain minimal transactional data may be temporarily retained strictly to comply with Indian statutory laws, taxation requirements, and fraud prevention:

  • Tax & Accounting Records: Completed order tax invoices are retained for the legally mandated period under GST and financial reporting laws (up to 180 days), after which they are permanently purged.
  • Strict Privacy Isolation: Any retained legal data is isolated, encrypted, restricted to compliance officers only, and will never be used for commercial, advertising, or profiling purposes.
