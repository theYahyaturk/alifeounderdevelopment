Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 • Platform: Alifeo Mobile Application & alifeo.in

Welcome to Alifeo (“we”, “our”, or “us”). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy governs our data practices across the Alifeo mobile application (available on Google Play Store) and our website at https://alifeo.in.

1. Introduction & Applicability

Alifeo is an Indian hyperlocal e-commerce and quick-commerce marketplace connecting consumers with local merchants, home decor artisans, grocery providers, and delivery partners across India.

By downloading, accessing, or using the Alifeo Android application or website, you agree to the collection and use of your information in accordance with this Privacy Policy, the Indian Information Technology Act (2000), IT Rules (2011), the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act (2023), and Google Play Developer Program Policies.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide, maintain, protect, and improve our shopping and hyperlocal delivery services:

A. Information You Provide to Us Directly

  • Personal Contact Details: Full name, verified mobile phone number, email address, and profile photo when you register or sign in.
  • Delivery Address Information: Street address, landmark, PIN code, and contact person names for doorstep order deliveries.
  • Order & Purchase Details: Items placed in cart, order history, transaction amounts, promotional codes applied, and customer service communications.
  • Identity Verification: Authentication credentials when you sign in via OTP, password, or third-party sign-in (e.g. Google Sign-In).

B. Location Information (Google Play Disclosure)

To enable core hyperlocal functionalities such as showing stores delivering to your area, accurate delivery fee estimates, and real-time delivery tracking, Alifeo collects your precise (GPS-based) and approximate (network-based) location:

  • While using the app (Foreground): To automatically pinpoint your delivery address and show available products in your immediate vicinity.
  • Delivery Partner Real-Time Tracking: When an active order is in transit, location is used to display live rider arrival updates.
Location Permission Transparency:

You can adjust or revoke location permissions at any time via your device settings. If disabled, you can still place orders by manually entering your delivery address and PIN code.

C. Device, Technical & Usage Information

  • Device Identifiers: Device manufacturer, hardware model, operating system version, and unique device IDs for app security and compatibility.
  • Push Notification Tokens: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) tokens to deliver essential transaction alerts (order placed, out for delivery, delivered).
  • Log & Crash Data: Diagnostic logs, performance metrics, and error traces to fix bugs and improve app reliability.

D. Payment Information

All electronic payments on Alifeo are processed directly by Reserve Bank of India (RBI) authorized, PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant payment gateways (e.g. Razorpay, Cashfree, UPI). Alifeo does not store your raw debit/credit card numbers, CVV, or UPI PINs on its servers.

3. How We Use Your Information

We process your data strictly for legitimate business and fulfillment purposes:

  • Order Processing & Delivery: Fulfilling purchases, assigning nearest delivery riders, and delivering orders to your chosen address.
  • Account Management & Customer Support: Managing your profile, order tracking, returns, inquiries, and technical troubleshooting.
  • Transaction Communications: Sending order confirmations, OTPs, delivery updates, and invoice receipts via SMS, WhatsApp, and email.
  • Safety & Fraud Prevention: Detecting fraudulent transactions, preventing fake accounts, verifying order integrity, and securing our systems.
  • Legal & Regulatory Compliance: Maintaining tax invoices as required under GST regulations and Indian financial laws.

4. Sharing & Disclosure of Information

No Selling of Personal Data:

Alifeo does NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third parties for advertising or commercial marketing purposes.

We only share minimal information with verified partners strictly necessary to complete your order:

  • Delivery Riders & Logistics Partners: We share your recipient name, delivery address, and contact number only during active order delivery so the rider can navigate and reach you.
  • Merchant Sellers: Information regarding ordered items, order IDs, and packaging instructions.
  • Authorized Technology Providers: Cloud infrastructure (Google Cloud Platform / Firebase), SMS gateway partners, and payment processors bound by strict confidentiality and data protection agreements.
  • Legal Authorities: Only when strictly required by applicable Indian law, court order, or government authority to prevent fraud or enforce platform legal terms.

5. Data Security & Storage

We employ industry-standard technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction:

  • All network communications between the mobile application, website, and backend servers are encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).
  • Access to personal data is restricted to authorized Alifeo personnel strictly on a need-to-know basis.
  • Data is stored in secure, ISO/IEC 27001 certified cloud data centers hosted in accordance with Indian data protection standards.

6. Your Rights & Permanent Account Deletion

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act) and Google Play User Data policies, you hold full rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to Access & Review: You may view your profile, address book, and order logs directly in the app.
  • Right to Rectification: You can update or correct your profile details anytime in account settings.
  • Right to Erasure (Account & Data Deletion): You have the absolute right to permanently delete your Alifeo account and all associated personal data.
Account & Data Deletion Portal:

You can request permanent account and data deletion at any time by visiting our dedicated deletion portal at:
https://alifeo.in/my-account/wpf-delete-account
Alternatively, you can delete your account directly inside the Alifeo mobile app under Account > Settings > Delete Account, or email us at alifeo.com@gmail.com.

7. Children’s Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 18 (“Minors”). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If a parent or guardian becomes aware that their child has provided us with personal information without parental consent, please contact our Grievance Officer immediately so we can promptly delete such data.

8. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website (alifeo.in) may use cookies and similar session storage technologies solely to maintain your shopping cart state, remember language/theme preferences, and ensure secure authentication. We do not use intrusive third-party cross-site ad tracking cookies.

9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our operational practices, app features, or regulatory requirements. When updates occur, we will update the “Effective Date” at the top of this document and notify active users via an in-app notice or website banner.
